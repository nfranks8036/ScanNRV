## Callouts
In unit-to-unit conversation, there is often a specific order and protocol to how to get the attention of another unit or dispatch. In this area, **dispatch is known as __NRV__**. Due to the variety of agencies, agency types, etc. in this area, these rules may not apply to every channel or agency; some of them do vary. 

**1) Unit calls another unit.**
They say the name of their unit first, *then* the unit they're trying to call.
-# \* Does not apply to Blacksburg Rescue, Christiansburg Rescue, Riner Rescue, or Montgomery County Medic units (M13-1, M13-2), where it's the opposite order.
"Tech 153, NRV." (Virginia Tech Police 153 to NRV)

**2) The receiving unit acknowledges the message.**
They may repeat the unit number they heard or they may just say "Go ahead".
"Tech 153, go ahead." (NRV replies with this phrasing)

**3) Information is passed along**
This is where the primary content of the message is transmitted.
"**10-38** [traffic stop] at Washington Street and Beamer Way, **10-28** [license plate] of Virginia ABC1234"
