
## Where to listen
You're able to listen to radio activity in many places:

1) **RdioScanner**
You are also able to listen to the scanner on RdioScanner (which is the software you see on the stage). This system is best for live (or close-to-live) listening. Find that here: <br>
https://radio.vtfirewatch.com/

2) **OpenMHz**
You are also able to listen to the scanner on the OpenMHz instance. This system is best for saving audio clips or creating events to group large amounts of radio traffic clips together. Find that here: <br>
https://openmhz.com/system/nrv911

3) **Firewatch @ Virginia Tech**
You are also able to listen to the scanner live in Firewatch at Virginia Tech (open for anybody!). This system is best for listening live with other people at the exact same moment. This Discord server also provides a live radio feed of user-contributed incidents and their summaries. Find that here: <br>
https://vtfirewatch.com (join Discord)

If you want to see all available resources regarding the radio feed, please visit
https://scannrv.com/
