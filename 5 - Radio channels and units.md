## Radio channels and units
There are several radio channels used in the New River Valley for units to communicate between each other and dispatch. Some of these channels have purposes that may not seem very obvious, so here is a list of all the channels, who uses them, and what units you're likely to hear. For Fire & EMS, a "primary ops" channel is a channel where you will hear dispatch tones (when NRV request a unit to respond to a call) and operations (ops, such as marking en-route, on-scene, etc.). For Fire & Rescue, the "county tac" section does not have these same dispatch tones, only operations. 

**__Law Enforcement__** <br>
- **MONT CO SHERIFF** - `MCSO` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: Montgomery ### <br>
- **CHRISTIANSBURG POLICE** - `CPD` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: Christiansburg ### <br>
- **BLACKSBURG POLICE** - `BPD` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: Blacksburg ### <br>
- **VIRGINIA TECH POLICE** - `VTPD` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: Tech ### (or Virginia Tech ###) <br>

**__Law Enforcement - Other__** <br>
- **MONT CO SHERIFF - JAIL** - `MCSO-Jail` (Jail Ops) <br>
   ↳ Used by the Sheriff's Office for communication within the jail <br>
- **MONT CO SHERIFF - COURTS** - `MCSO-Courts` (Court Ops) <br>
   ↳ Used by the Sheriff's Office for communication within the courthouse <br>

**__Fire & EMS - Dispatch__** <br>
- **MONT CO F/R DISPATCH** - `Fire/Rescue 1` (Countywide Fire/Rescue dispatch only) <br>

**__Fire & EMS - Primary Ops__** <br>
- **BLACKSBURG RESCUE** - `BRS 1` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: 5xx <br>
- **BLACKSBURG FIRE** - `BFD 1` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: 1xx <br>
- **VIRGINIA TECH RESCUE** - `VTRS 1` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: 15xx <br>
- **CHRISTIANSBURG FIRE** - `CFD 1` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: 8xx <br>
- **LONGSHOP-MCOY FIRE/RESCUE** - `LNGSHP 1` (Dispatch + Ops) <br>
   ↳ Units: Rescue 3xx / Fire 6xx <br>

**__Fire & EMS - County Tac__** <br>
- **SOUTH 3** - Christiansburg Rescue, Riner Rescue, Montgomery County ops (no dispatch tones) <br>
   ↳ Units: 9xx / 11xx / 13xx <br>
- **SOUTH 5** - Overflow / secondary channel <br>
- **SOUTH 7** - Elliston Fire ops (no dispatch tones) <br>
   ↳ Units: 2xx <br>
- **SOUTH 9** - Overflow / secondary channel <br>
- **SOUTH 11** - Riner Fire ops (no dispatch tones) <br>
   ↳ Units: 4xx <br>

**__Public Services__**
- **BLACKSBURG TRANSIT** - `BT Fixed Route` + `BT Access` (combined operations channels) <br>
   ↳ Units: Bus unit numbers, "Base" for contacting Fixed Route dispatcher, "Access" for contacting the Access dispatcher <br>
- **CHRISTIANSBURG PUBLIC WORKS** - `CPW` (ops) <br>
   ↳ Used by the Town of Christiansburg to coordinate public works efforts, such as snow removal <br>
   ↳ Units: 14xx <br>
