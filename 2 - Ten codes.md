## Ten codes
In the New River Valley, police agencies and Blacksburg Transit use ten-codes for efficient communication over the radio. Here are some common ones that you may hear in this area. Note that ten-codes are best-guesses only, as police and dispatch agencies do not tend to release their list of ten codes. In this area, fire & rescue do not routinely use ten-codes (apart from `10-4`).

__**Police Ten Codes**__ <br>
`10-0` - Caution <br>
`10-3` - Clear the Air / Emergency Traffic Only <br>
`10-4` - Acknowledgement (OK) <br>
`10-6` - Busy, Unless Emergent <br>
`10-7` - Out of Service <br>
`10-8` - In Service (usually said by units when returning to patrol, or when they're finished with a call) <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;* Often police will clear with an "incident disposition", which is a numeric value that tells dispatch *why* the incident is closed. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;* These numeric values are difficult to assign meaning to because they're *extremely* agency-dependent (even more than ten codes)! <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;* Example: "10-8 16" (means in-service, assist agency) <br>
`10-9` - Say Again <br>
`10-10` - Fight <br>
`10-12` - Standby (Wait) <br>
`10-16` - Domestic Disturbance <br>
`10-17` - Reporting Party / Caller / Complainant <br>
`10-19` - Respond to Station / At Station <br>
`10-20` - Location (What is your location) <br>
`10-21` - Call (#) by phone <br>
`10-22` - Cancel / Disregard <br>
`10-23` - Arrived on Scene <br>
`10-25` - Meet at... <br>
`10-27` - Driver's License / ID Check <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;* NRV will respond with: `[Licensed status]` / `[Warrants or Other Restrictions]` <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;* A person who is licensed and has no warrants may return "Licensed, negative 29" (see: `10-29`) <br>
`10-28` - Vehicle Registration Info <br>
`10-29` - Check for Warrants / Wanted Status <br>
`10-32` - Gun / Individual with Gun <br>
`10-33` - Emergency / Need Emergency Assistance <br>
`10-37` - Suspicious Vehicle <br>
`10-38` - Traffic Stop <br>
`10-41` - Beginning Duty Shift <br>
`10-42` - Ending Duty Shift <br>
`10-43` - Information <br>
`10-50` - Motor Vehicle Crash <br>
`10-51` - Tow Truck Needed <br>
`10-52` - Ambulance Needed <br>
`10-55` - Intoxicated Driver <br>
`10-56` - Intoxicated Individual <br>
`10-60` - In the Area <br>
`10-75` - In Contact with... <br>
`10-76` - Enroute to... <br>
`10-80` - In Pursuit <br>
`10-95` - Subject in Custody <br>
`10-96` - Mentally Ill Subject <br>
`10-99` - Subject is Wanted / has Warrants <br>
`10-100` - Suspicious <br>

Transit often uses a lot of the same ten codes at police, with some exceptions: <br>

`10-8` - Back on Bus <br>
`10-11` - Standby / Wait <br>
`10-13` - Wheelchair Passenger <br>
`10-14` - Passenger <br>
`10-15` - Visually Impaired Passenger <br>
`10-17` - Left Passenger Behind <br>
`10-18` - Continue On Route <br>
`10-38` - Request a Supervisor <br>
`10-40` - Vehicle On Schedule <br>
`10-41` - Vehicle Ahead of Schedule <br>
`10-42` - Vehicle Behind Schedule <br>
`10-45` - Information <br>
`10-46` - Full Passenger Load... Turning Passengers Away <br>
`10-51` - Collision... Bus Involved <br>
`10-52` - Need Ambulance at... <br>
`10-53` - Need Police at.... <br>
`10-74` - Negative;  No <br>
`10-83` - Looking for Lost Item (w/ Description) <br>
