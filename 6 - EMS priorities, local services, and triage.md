## EMS priorities, local services, and triage

### __Priorities__
When an EMS call is dispatched, NRV often includes a priority level in the dispatch message. This priority level was chosen by an emergency medical dispatch (EMD) software after the dispatcher asks specific question. This software also includes a "Chief Complaint", which is the 1 of 36 reasons for dispatch you may hear ("Bleeding / Lacerations", "Falls", "Overdose / Ingestion", etc.).

**Omega Response:** BLS truck, no lights or sirens; the lowest priority in this system <br>
**Alpha Response:** BLS truck, no lights or sirens; determined to be non-life threatening <br>
**Bravo Response:** BLS truck, lights and sirens; determined that a BLS truck can handle the call <br>
**Charlie Response:** ALS truck, lights and sirens; possibly life threatening condition <br>
**Delta Response:** ALS truck w/ supervisor, lights and sirens; life threatening condition <br>
**Echo Response:** ALS truck w/ supervisor, lights and sirens; full cardiac arrest or imminent death <br>

The response determination varies *a lot* per agency, so these response guidelines provide a general idea of acuity but may not represent the actual response received. Read more about the Medical Priority Dispatch System [by clicking here](<https://www.angelfire.com/nc/neurosurgery/Priority.pdf>).

### __Local hospitals__
**Local hospitals you may hear on the radio include:**
- Owned by: HCA Healthcare Virginia *(for-profit structured institution)*
    - **LGHM:** LewisGale Hospital Montgomery
        - Capabilities: Level III trauma center
    - Transport time: Blacksburg: ~10m; Christiansburg: ~12m
  - **LGHM Christiansburg Free-standing ER:** LewisGale Hospital Montgomery Christiansburg
        - Capabilities: Generalized emergency room
    - Transport time: Blacksburg: ~23m; Christiansburg: ~3m
  - **LGH Salem:** LewisGale Hospital Salem
        - Capabilities: Level II trauma center, stroke center, STEMI/cardiac center, level II NICU center, labor & delievery, psych / mental health services
    - Transport time: Blacksburg: ~51m; Christiansburg: ~42m
- Owned by: Carilion Clinic *(non-profit integrated healthcare system, heavily invested in research)*
    - **NRVMC:** Carilion New River Valley Medical Center Radford
        - Capabilities: Level III trauma center, NICU center, labor & delievery
    - Transport time: Blacksburg: ~24m; Christiansburg: ~11m
  - **RMH:** Roanoke Memorial Hospital
        - Capabilities: Level I trauma center, level I pediatric trauma center, stroke center, STEMI/cardiac center, level IIIB NICU center, labor & delievery, psych / mental health services
    - Transport time: Blacksburg: ~54m; Christiansburg: ~46m

\* Measuring transport times from Blacksburg at: North Main St / Jackson St NW
\* Measuring transport times from Christiansburg at: Franklin St / E Main St
**Times do vary by a significant amount depending on location in town**, this is just a general idea!

### __Triage codes__
- When describing a patient's condition, EMS may use one of a few triage color codes after a quick assessment, including:
    - :green_circle: GREEN / MINOR = Patients who are able to follow instructions and walk
  - :yellow_circle: YELLOW / DELAYED = Patients who need transport but can be delayed to prioritize more serious patients
  - :red_circle: RED / IMMEDIATE = Patients who have a life-threatening condition who need immediate transport
  - :black_circle: BLACK / EXPECTANT = Patients who are unlikely to survive their injuries or are already dead
- Learn more about EMS triage [by clicking here](https://www.pocketprep.com/posts/mastering-start-triage-for-ems-professionals/).

### __Life-Guard__
Life-Guard is the local air ambulance service provided by Carilion Clinic. They service the New River Valley and will transport critical patients to the local Level I trauma center, Roanoke Memorial Hospital, when time is of the essence. Learn more at [this behind-the-scenes look](<https://www.carilionclinic.org/health-and-wellness/article/behind-scenes-look-life-guard>) at Life-Guard.
